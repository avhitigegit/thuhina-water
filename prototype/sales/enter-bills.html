Rules

  • Exchange: the customer pays only the water price.
  • Fewer empties than filled → the difference becomes bottles owed (note required).
  • More empties than filled → clears empties left at the door, then bottles owed.
  • Left at door: tick the phone agreement and add a note. The empties are collected next trip – not a shortage.
  • The system gives every bill its own number (B000…). The paper bill number is kept only to stop the same paper bill being entered twice.
  • Saving moves the customer's next delivery date forward.
