How to use

  1. Start at login.html and pick a demo user (any password).
  2. Use the Role switcher in the top bar to see how the sidebar and access change for Admin, Accountant and Delivery Staff.
  3. Demo data is stored in your browser (localStorage), so a bill entered on one screen updates the customer profile, delivery list, stock and dashboard.
  4. Reset demo data (top bar) restores the original sample data.
  5. The business date is fixed at Friday 02/10/2026 so the sample delivery list and aging always line up.

Suggested demo path

  1. Delivery Schedule – customers due today, incl. one who was missed last week.
  2. Enter Bills – the customers due on the selected date are listed automatically; type the paper bill numbers with the keyboard; try a shortage, a "Left at door" row and a duplicate bill number.
  3. Customers – click a customer to see bottles, balance and history update.
  4. Purchasing – open QR-2026-013 → choose a quotation → create PO → approve → receive goods.
  5. First purchase for the new customer Priyanka Rathnayake, with an American Water bottle.
  6. Agreed prices – Ceyknit Garments pays an agreed Rs. 270 for 20L (Factory standard Rs. 300); click Prices to change it. All agreed prices are listed under Bottles & Products → Prices.
  7. Customer Quotations – open QT-2026-001 (accepted) → Register as customer: the quoted Rs. 300 becomes their agreed price.
  8. Expenses – add a month's bills; see net profit in Reports → Profit.
  9. Billing – generate September invoices → receive a payment → check aging.
